1.The short version

Arrow Maze has no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or exact location. Your game progress stays on your phone.

2.What stays on your device

Your level progress, scores, settings (sound, music, volume, larger text), hints, shields, daily streak and rewards are saved locally on your device. We cannot see them. They are removed if you clear the game's data or uninstall it.

The backup code feature creates a code on your device. It is only shared if you choose to copy it somewhere.

3.Ads and the Remove Ads purchase

Arrow Maze may show ads and may use third-party services such as Google AdMob or Google Play services. These services can collect anonymous or device-level information, such as advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with the app, to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Their use of this data follows their own policies, for example Google's Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalisation in your phone's settings by resetting or opting out of your advertising ID.

Arrow Maze has one in-app purchase: Remove Ads. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the game that the purchase is active, so ads can be switched off.

4.Who we share data with

We never sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers that help run the game (such as advertising partners), when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and our players.

5.Children

Arrow Maze is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Younger players should play with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

6.Your choices

  • Clear the game's storage or uninstall it to remove your saved progress.
  • Reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you live in the EEA, UK, California or a similar region, you may have rights to access, correct or delete data held by us or our partners. Email nkbussines99@gmail.com to make a request.

7.Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no app or network is completely secure.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to play after an update means you accept it.
