No account, no sign-in

Word Explorer does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal detail to play. Your level progress, coins, gems, and settings are saved automatically on your own device.

Because there's no sign-in, we have no way to identify you personally, and we don't ask you to.

What information exists at all

We don't operate our own servers that collect gameplay data. The only information generated comes from the store your device uses to install and run the app, and from the two categories of service described below.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personally identifiable information.

Services we rely on

Word Explorer may use a small set of outside services to keep the app running, show occasional advertising, and process in-app purchases. These services can collect limited technical information under their own privacy policies — we don't control this data and never see it tied to your identity.

ServicePurposeWhat it may see
App store (Google Play / App Store)Distributing the app, handling in-app purchasesDevice and purchase information, per the store's own policy
Advertising networksShowing ads that support free playDevice advertising identifiers, general (non-precise) location, ad interaction data
Analytics / crash reportingUnderstanding crashes and app performanceDevice model, OS version, app version, crash logs

None of these services receive your name or email from us, because we never collect that information in the first place.

Where your progress lives

Your levels, coins, gems, and settings are stored locally on your device. If the app offers a cloud-backup option through your app store account, that backup is managed by the store itself, not by us — uninstalling the app or clearing its data will remove your local progress.

In-app purchases

If you choose to buy coins, gems, or an ad-removal option, that purchase is handled entirely by the app store's payment system. We never see or store your payment card details — the store passes us only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded so the app can unlock the item.

Children's privacy

Word Explorer is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app has no sign-in and no chat or messaging features, there's no place for a child to enter personal details even if they wanted to.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app in some way we haven't anticipated, contact us at the email below and we'll act promptly to remove it.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of interest-based ads in your device's system settings at any time.
  • You can clear the app's local data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on your device.
  • You can purchase an ad-removal option, if offered, to stop seeing advertising within the app.

Keeping things secure

Because gameplay data stays on your device and we don't run servers that store personal information, there's no central database of player data to protect. Any information handled by third-party services (app stores, ad networks) is secured under their own respective safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Effective" date at the top of this page and, for meaningful changes, note the change in the app's release notes. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
