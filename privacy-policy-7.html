Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Word Explorer (the "App"), developed by FSD Studio Games ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the App. If you have any questions, contact us at nkbussines99@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Word Explorer itself does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to play. We do not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information from you.

Optional Cloud Save (Firebase)

If you choose to sign in (for example, to save your progress across devices), the App uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore (Google services) to store your game progress. This is entirely optional — if you don't sign in, no cloud account information is created and your progress stays only on your device. Firebase's handling of this data is governed by Google's Firebase Privacy and Security policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information automatically to serve and measure ads, such as device identifiers, advertising ID, general device information, and approximate location. We do not have direct access to this data — it is collected and processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data policies. You can manage your ad personalization settings through your device's settings (e.g., "Reset advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed through the Google Play Billing system. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Any data handled by third-party services described above (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play Billing) is subject to those providers' own privacy policies, linked above.

Your Choices

  • You can play the App without signing in, avoiding cloud data storage entirely.
  • You can purchase "Remove Ads" to stop ad display and related ad data collection through AdMob.
  • You can request deletion of your cloud save data by contacting us at nkbussines99@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at nkbussines99@gmail.com.
