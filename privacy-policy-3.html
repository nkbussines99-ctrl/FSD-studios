Privacy Policy

App: Word Explorer  •  Developer: FSD Studios  •  Contact: nkbussines99@gmail.com  •  Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Word Explorer ("the app," "we," "our") is a word-search puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We don't run our own servers, and the app does not send your gameplay data anywhere.

Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal information ourselves — we have no server of our own. Specifically:

  • No account with us. The app itself has no login system and never asks you for an email, phone number, or contact details.
  • No location data. The app never accesses or requests your device location.
  • No camera or photo access. Player avatars are chosen from a built-in set of preset characters — the app never requests camera or photo library access.
  • Nothing gameplay-related is sent to us. We don't run a server, so we have no way to see your progress, in-game name, or settings — see below for the two places that data actually lives.

Where Your Progress Is Stored

The app saves your progress in two possible places, described below. Neither of them is a server we run — we don't have access to either one.

On your device (always). By default, the app saves the following locally, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Game progress (coins, gems, unlocked levels, stars earned, words found)
  • Daily reward streak
  • The display name and preset avatar you choose in-app
  • Whether you've purchased "Remove Ads"

This local copy is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Your Google account (optional). Tapping "Save your progress" in Settings will, the first time you do it, ask you to sign in with Google Play Games. If you choose to sign in, the same progress data listed above is also saved to your own Google Play Games account via Google's Saved Games service, so it can follow you to a new device or survive a reinstall. This is optional — the app works fully with local-only save if you never tap that sign-in prompt. Once you're signed in, that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, since it's stored on Google's infrastructure, not ours; we still don't receive a copy of it ourselves.

Advertising

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded-video ads) served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data — such as advertising ID, general device information, and approximate location derived from IP address — to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We do not receive or store this advertising data ourselves — it passes directly between your device and Google.

If you purchase "Remove Ads," ads stop showing and AdMob is no longer used beyond confirming that one-time purchase.

You can generally opt out of personalized/interest-based advertising through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"), which limits — but does not entirely stop — the data used to select ads shown to you.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases of virtual currency (Coins and Gems) and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. These are processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We do not receive or store your payment details — card numbers, billing address, etc. — that's handled directly by Google under its own Privacy Policy.
